# ProtectionScale

Dogeash's own five-level scale saying, in plain words, what a dog will do. It appears on every K9 file, in filters, and on the Protection levels page.

| Level | Name | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Alert companion | Warns you; a family dog first |
| 2 | Deterrent | Visible, confident, fully controllable |
| 3 | Property guard | Patrols and holds a perimeter |
| 4 | Family protection | Protects people on command, releases on command |
| 5 | Estate and executive | Titled dogs for large estates and experienced owners |

- Five 8px bars, filled in `action`, empty in `line`, with "Level N · Name" above.
- Always the word with the bars; never colour alone.
- Sellers choose the level; Dogeash checks it at kennel inspection.
