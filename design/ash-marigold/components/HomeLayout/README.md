# HomeLayout

Straight to the point, in white, ash, charcoal and marigold blocks, with a few loud details.

1. Header, then a thin **hazard stripe**.
2. **Hero** on white: a mega headline (Archivo 125% width, 900) where "Checked" sits on a marigold block and "by us." is outlined. Search with a marigold button. On the right, the **patrol map** in its night version (charcoal ground, marigold route) with a slowly turning **"Verified kennels only" sticker**.
3. **Promise strip**: charcoal pill with the four guarantees.
4. **Big numbers**: three 104px figures on white with thick black rules (example figures until real data exists).
5. **What are you protecting?** on ash with a mega heading.
6. **Ready now**: K9 files. Hovering or focusing a card slides up a marigold **"Open K9 file →"** bar over the photo.
7. **Protection scale** with 72px numerals.
8. **How it works** with the dashed patrol line.

Mega headings outline one word at most. Under reduced motion the sticker stops turning, the photo bar appears instantly instead of sliding, and nothing rises in.
