# PropertyPicker

The fastest way in: four big colour tiles that ask "What are you protecting?" and jump to dogs at the right protection level.

- Tiles: Home (`marigold`), Compound (`ash`), Estate (`ash-soft`), Business (`surface`). Each has an icon, the property type, and the suggested levels and dog count in mono.
- Lives in the Home hero and at the top of Browse for first-time visitors.
- Tiles lift 3px on hover; they are buttons and take keyboard focus.
